Gold, precious metals and stones

For: Dealers in precious metals and stones, refiners and bullion traders.

The standard: Recommendations 22 and 23 require dealers in precious metals and dealers in precious stones to apply customer due diligence and report suspicious transactions when they engage in a cash transaction with a customer at or above the designated threshold. Under Recommendation 28, a supervisor or a self-regulatory body monitors them for AML/CFT compliance on a risk-sensitive basis. Under Recommendations 6 and 7, countries require every natural and legal person to freeze, without delay and without prior notice, the funds or other assets of designated persons and entities.
