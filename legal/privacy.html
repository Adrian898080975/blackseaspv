Privacy.

Last updated: 7 June 2026

Who we are

The data controller is Black Sea SPV. For any matter relating to this notice or to your personal data, contact us at operations@blackseaspv.com.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not profile visitors. We measure traffic with a privacy-first, cookieless analytics service (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that counts page views in aggregate without storing anything on your device and without identifying you. Because nothing non-essential is stored on your device, this site does not show a cookie-consent banner.

What we collect

When you contact us through the website form, by email, or in connection with a prospective or active engagement, we may collect:

  • Name, role and organisation
  • Email address and the market or sector you mention
  • A description of the matter you are seeking advice on
  • Any additional information you choose to share with us in correspondence

The contact form is delivered to us by a third-party form processor (Formspree), which transmits your message to our inbox and does not use it for any other purpose.

Why we collect it

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your enquiry and providing the advisory services you request
  • Performing client due diligence as required by anti-money-laundering rules
  • Maintaining records of professional engagements as required by applicable law
  • Communicating about the engagement, including invoicing and reporting

Lawful basis

The lawful bases on which we process your data are: (a) your consent, where you provide it; (b) the performance of a contract or steps prior to entering into one; (c) compliance with our legal obligations; and (d) our legitimate interests in operating a professional advisory practice, balanced against your rights.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry correspondence for up to 24 months from the last interaction. Engagement records are retained for the period required by applicable professional and accounting law, typically 10 years from the end of the relevant financial year.

Who processes it for us

We do not sell your data. We share it only:

  • With our hosting and content-delivery provider, and our cookieless analytics provider, who process technical data such as server logs to deliver and secure the site
  • With our form provider (Formspree), to deliver messages you send through the website
  • With partner counsel engaged on your matter, with your knowledge
  • With professional service providers (accounting, IT, banking) on a need-to-know basis under written confidentiality
  • Where required by a competent authority or court order

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to object to processing, subject to the conditions set out in the EU General Data Protection Regulation and applicable data-protection law. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at operations@blackseaspv.com.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how we have handled your personal data, you may lodge a complaint with the data-protection authority in your jurisdiction. In Slovakia this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov).
