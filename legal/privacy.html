Privacy.

Last updated: 19 May 2026

Who we are

The data controller is Black Sea SPV s.r.o., a company registered in the Slovak Republic with registered office in Bratislava. For any matter relating to this notice or to your personal data, please contact adrian@blackseaspv.com.

What we collect

When you contact us through the website form, by email, or in connection with a prospective or active engagement, we may collect:

  • Name, role and organisation
  • Email address and other contact details you provide
  • A description of the matter you are seeking advice on
  • Any additional information you choose to share with us in correspondence

We do not use website tracking, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics on this site beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the page.

Why we collect it

We use your information for the following purposes only:

  • Responding to your enquiry and providing the advisory services you request
  • Performing client due diligence as required by anti-money-laundering rules
  • Maintaining records of professional engagements as required by Slovak law
  • Communicating about the engagement, including invoicing and reporting

Lawful basis

The lawful bases on which we process your data are: (a) your consent, where you provide it; (b) the performance of a contract or steps prior to entering into one; (c) compliance with our legal obligations; and (d) our legitimate interests in operating a professional advisory practice, balanced against your rights.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry correspondence for up to 24 months from the last interaction. Engagement records are retained for the period required by Slovak professional and accounting law, typically 10 years from the end of the relevant financial year.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only:

  • With partner counsel engaged on your matter, with your knowledge
  • With professional service providers (accounting, IT, banking) on a need-to-know basis under written confidentiality
  • Where required by a competent authority or court order

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to object to processing, subject to the conditions set out in the EU General Data Protection Regulation and Slovak data protection law. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at adrian@blackseaspv.com.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how we have handled your personal data, you may lodge a complaint with the Slovak Office for Personal Data Protection (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR).
