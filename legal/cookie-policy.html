What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly and provide basic usage information to the site operator.

Cookies we use

Essential cookies

Required for the website to function. They cannot be disabled without affecting core functionality. No personal data is stored beyond what is strictly necessary.

Analytics cookies (optional)

Help us understand how visitors interact with our website — for example, which pages are visited most. This data is aggregated and anonymised where possible.

Your choices

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

Third-party cookies

We may use limited third-party services (e.g. hosting or analytics providers) that set their own cookies, subject to their own privacy policies.
