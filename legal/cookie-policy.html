Cookie Policy

Last updated: 15.02.2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and provide usage information to site owners.

Cookies we use

Essential cookies

These are required for the website to function. They cannot be disabled without affecting core functionality. No personal data is stored beyond what is strictly necessary.

Analytics cookies (optional)

These help us understand how visitors interact with our website — for example, which pages are visited most. This data is aggregated and anonymised where possible.

Your choices

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

Third-party cookies

We may use limited third-party services (e.g., hosting or analytics providers) that set their own cookies. These are subject to their own privacy policies.
