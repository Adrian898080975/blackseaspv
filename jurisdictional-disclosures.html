Jurisdictional disclosures.

Last updated: 19 May 2026

About this notice

This page sets out information about Black Sea SPV s.r.o. (“Black Sea”) and the regulatory perimeter of its services. It is published in line with general professional disclosure practice and does not constitute legal, tax, investment or other regulated advice.

Entity and contact

Black Sea SPV s.r.o. is a company registered in the Slovak Republic with registered office in Bratislava. Correspondence and enquiries should be directed to adrian@blackseaspv.com.

Nature of services

Black Sea provides advisory services in the following areas:

  • Fund formation support for fund managers raising into EU defense and dual-use opportunities
  • Cross-border regulatory positioning for crypto, fintech and digital-asset operators
  • Compliance architecture and assurance for regulated and licensable activities

These services are advisory in nature. Black Sea does not, on its own account, undertake any regulated activity that would require authorisation in the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States or in any other jurisdiction. In particular:

  • Black Sea is not authorised as a credit institution, payment institution, electronic money institution, investment firm, fund manager, crypto-asset service provider, or any equivalent licensed entity.
  • Black Sea does not custody, hold, transmit or trade client funds or client crypto-assets.
  • Black Sea does not issue or distribute investment recommendations within the meaning of MAR or equivalent regimes.

Engagement of licensed counsel

Where a client matter requires regulated legal opinion, signed counsel, or admission to a regulator’s register, Black Sea retains partner counsel licensed in the relevant jurisdiction. Engagement of such counsel is disclosed to the client in writing and any associated fees are quoted up front.

No investment advice

Nothing on this website, in any document prepared by Black Sea, or in any communication with Black Sea, constitutes an offer, solicitation, recommendation or invitation to buy, sell or hold any security, fund interest, crypto-asset or other financial instrument. Black Sea does not provide personal investment advice and clients are responsible for taking their own decisions, including where necessary by reference to licensed advisers in the relevant jurisdiction.

Conflicts of interest

Black Sea operates a conflict register and discloses material conflicts to prospective and existing clients in writing prior to engagement. Where a conflict cannot be appropriately managed, Black Sea declines the engagement.

Anti-money laundering

Black Sea operates internal AML procedures appropriate to its advisory activity and may decline engagement, or terminate an existing engagement, where it cannot satisfy itself as to the source of funds, the identity of beneficial owners, or the lawful nature of the underlying business. Black Sea reports as required under Slovak law and cooperates with competent authorities in accordance with applicable disclosure obligations.

Sanctions

Black Sea does not act for sanctioned persons or entities, and screens prospective engagements against EU, UK, UN, and OFAC sanctions lists and equivalent international measures.

Limitation of liability

Engagements are governed by written agreement specifying scope, fees, jurisdiction and the limitation of liability applicable to each engagement. In the absence of a written agreement, no advisory relationship is created by use of this website or by initial enquiry communications.

Changes

This notice may be amended from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
