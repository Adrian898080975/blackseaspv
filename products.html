Build

Licensing & new-regime build

You are racing a VASP, CASP, PVARA, DPMS or DNFBP deadline. The licence file can be assembled by advisers. The programme behind it cannot be faked, and it is what supervision tests. We build it before you file, working alongside your counsel who own the application.

The framework
  • Business-wide ML/TF risk assessment
  • Policies, controls and governance
  • Customer risk-assessment methodology
The controls
  • CDD, EDD and beneficial ownership
  • Sanctions and PEP screening
  • Transaction monitoring and the Travel Rule
The evidence
  • FIU registration and goAML reporting
  • A named, fit-and-proper MLRO
  • Examiner-ready documentation
