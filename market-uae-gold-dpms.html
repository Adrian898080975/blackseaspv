Gold, precious metals and stones in the United Arab Emirates

Who is the AML/CFT supervisor for gold, precious metals and stones in the United Arab Emirates?

In the United Arab Emirates, the supervisor depends on where the business is set up:

WhereSupervisor
Onshore and the commercial free zonesThe Ministry of Economy and Tourism

For: Dealers in precious metals and stones, refiners and bullion traders.

Reporting: Suspicious transaction reports go to the UAE FIU alone. Virtual asset firms and DNFBPs register on goAML, the FIU's reporting system.

The MLRO seat: In the DIFC and in ADGM, the MLRO must be resident in the UAE. In ADGM the FSRA approves the appointment.

Recent change: Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 repealed Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018, the previous AML/CFT law. A programme written to the 2018 law needs mapping to the new texts.

The standard: Recommendations 22 and 23 require dealers in precious metals and dealers in precious stones to apply customer due diligence and report suspicious transactions when they engage in a cash transaction with a customer at or above the designated threshold. Under Recommendation 28, a supervisor or a self-regulatory body monitors them for AML/CFT compliance on a risk-sensitive basis. Under Recommendations 6 and 7, countries require every natural and legal person to freeze, without delay and without prior notice, the funds or other assets of designated persons and entities.

What we build: customer and supplier due diligence; supply chain checks on origin and transport; cash transaction controls; sanctions screening of counterparties; and suspicious transaction reporting.
