Payments and MSBs

For: Payment institutions, electronic money issuers, remittance providers and money service businesses.

The standard: Recommendation 14 requires providers of money or value transfer services to be licensed or registered and monitored for AML/CFT compliance, and to include their agents in their AML/CFT programme and monitor them. Under Recommendation 16, the required originator and beneficiary information goes with a payment or value transfer and stays with it through the payment chain. A provider that controls both sides of a transfer considers both before deciding whether to file a suspicious transaction report, and files it in any country affected.
