Case studies

Problems we have solved properly.

Representative mandates, anonymised at the client's request. The pattern is consistent: a real deadline or a real finding, a defined scope, and a programme an examiner can actually test. We will walk through specifics on a call.

BuildVASPKazakhstan · AIFC
A first-time AIFC manager, weeks from a submission window
A new manager needed to raise into dual-use technology, but a round of AFSA amendments had invalidated key compliance documentation just as the regulator window approached.
Challenge
Approved-person and AML documentation no longer matched the amended rules, with no time to start over.
Outcome
Framework redesigned and the submission filed inside the window. A fractional MLRO was retained for ongoing supervision.
BuildCryptoNigeria
A custodian days from a central-bank deadline
A digital-asset custodian under SEC ARIP had a central-bank AML deadline approaching and no implementation roadmap. The previous adviser had focused on the licence and missed the obligation entirely.
Challenge
A full AML implementation roadmap and monitoring architecture, with the deadline measured in days.
Outcome
Roadmap and submission package delivered and filed with the central bank ahead of the deadline.
RunCryptoUAE
Two regulators, one framework, no contradiction
An operator with ADGM and VARA entities needed a single AML and CFT framework that satisfied both regulators at once, with a federal proliferation-financing decree adding a third layer.
Challenge
Reconcile three sets of obligations into one architecture without internal contradiction.
Outcome
A unified framework across both entities, the MLRO function documented and certified, and a quarterly review retainer ongoing.
RemediateDNFBPGulf
A fined firm with a re-inspection on the calendar
A professional firm had been penalised for the absence of a business risk assessment, a client-risk methodology and adequate due diligence, with the regulator expected back.
Challenge
Close the exact gaps the regulator named and evidence a programme that actually runs.
Outcome
Risk assessment, methodology, screening and CDD rebuilt as a fixed-scope remediation, with an independent assurance file for the return visit.
RemediateGold & DPMSDubai
A refiner rebuilding after a suspension
A precious-metals refiner under new ownership needed to rebuild its responsible-sourcing and AML programme to standard after a period of suspension, with accreditation on the line.
Challenge
A credible, audit-ready programme covering both AML and responsible sourcing, fast.
Outcome
Programme rebuilt to international standard with source-of-funds and supply-chain controls, ready for re-audit.
BuildPayments / MSBGhana
A remittance operator with partnerships suspended
A money-transfer operator had its inward-remittance partnerships suspended after the central bank flagged the channel and correspondent controls.
Challenge
Rebuild partner and channel due diligence to a standard the central bank would accept before the suspension could lift.
Outcome
Channel and correspondent controls rebuilt and packaged as a re-application file for the regulator.
