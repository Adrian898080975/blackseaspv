Where we work.

Three areas, defined by the structural and regulatory problems our clients bring us. We do not consult outside them.

EU · Defense Capital

Fund formation for emerging managers.

SICAV · AIF MFSA · CSSF · AFM Fractional CCO LP readiness

Defense capital is moving. The EU Defense Roadmap, the proposed €1B Fund-of-Funds, and the Defense Equity Facility are creating space for a new generation of fund managers raising into defense-tech, dual-use and resilience-grade infrastructure. Most of them are first- or second-time GPs.

The capital exists. The structuring expertise is scarce. That gap is where we work.

What we do

  • Structure design, SICAV, AIF, parallel vehicles, feeder structures
  • Regulator engagement and submission support (MFSA, CSSF, AFM and equivalents)
  • Fractional CCO mandate during launch and first 18 months of operation
  • LP onboarding readiness, data room, AML / KYC framework, side-letter standardisation
  • Documentation review, PPM, side letters, subscription agreements

Who this is for

First-time managers raising €30M to €250M. Family offices considering an allocation programme rather than direct deals. Defense-tech founders raising bridge or Series A who need fund-grade compliance posture for institutional rounds.

Engagement

Project-based, typically 60 to 180 days for a complete fund formation. Diagnostic and scoping in two weeks. Fixed price. We retain partner counsel in the relevant jurisdiction for matters that require licensed legal opinion.

Frontier Markets

Cross-border regulatory positioning.

Nigeria ARIP AIFC AFSA Azerbaijan UBO screening

New crypto and fintech frameworks are coming live across markets where international advisory bench is thin. Nigeria’s SEC ARIP under ISA 2025 is live with hard deadlines. AIFC’s AFSA framework amendments take effect 1 January 2026 with a six-month transition. Azerbaijan is twelve months ahead of its own licensing wave.

Local counsel knows local rules. International counsel knows global rules. The hard work is the seam where the two have to fit together, and that is what we do.

What we do

  • ARIP gap analysis for crypto operators with Nigerian-customer exposure
  • AIFC AFSA transition advisory, Capital Markets, Digital Assets, Crowdfunding
  • Azerbaijan pre-licensing positioning for fintech and payment operators
  • Multi-jurisdiction structuring, entity placement, treaty mapping, banking access
  • UBO and sanctions screening using World-Check class tools

Who this is for

Crypto exchanges and VASPs with customer footprint in new framework jurisdictions. Fintech and payments operators positioning ahead of licensing waves. Family offices and funds with frontier-market exposure that needs an EU-side compliance read.

Engagement

Gap analysis in two weeks. Full advisory mandates depend on jurisdiction and scope. Ongoing retainer for clients in active licensing.

Regulatory Advisory

Compliance architecture & assurance.

AML / KYC MiCA Due diligence Regulator response

For operators who already hold licences, or are about to, and need EU-grade compliance infrastructure. The work that turns a regulatory permission into a functioning compliance practice.

Where law firms write the policy and most consultancies leave you with a deck, we build the operating framework that an examiner can actually walk through.

What we do

  • AML / KYC framework design, policy, procedure, controls, evidence
  • MiCA readiness audit and remediation roadmap
  • Counterparty and UBO due diligence on onboarding and ongoing review
  • Regulator response support, questions, findings, remediation plans
  • Compliance officer mentoring during first 6 to 18 months of operation

Who this is for

CASPs and EMIs transitioning under MiCA. Multi-jurisdiction VASPs needing one master framework with jurisdiction-specific overlays. Funds onboarding LPs in regulated channels. Operators receiving regulator findings who need experienced help responding.
