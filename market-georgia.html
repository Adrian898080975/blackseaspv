Who supervises what in Georgia

Georgia splits financial-crime supervision across several bodies, not one desk. Some activity is licensed, some only registered, some sits in a sector with no dedicated regulator. Which body holds your file, whether you are licensed or registered, and what it will actually ask for: that is the first thing we map.

  • NBG, the National Bank: VASPs, payments and MSB, banks and financial institutions, investment funds.
  • MoF, the Ministry of Finance: dealing in precious stones and metals.
  • Revenue Service: gaming and gambling, and export permits.
  • NAPR / MoJ, the Bar and SARAS: real estate gatekeeping and the DNFBP professions.
  • MoD: defence and dual-use export control.
  • FMS: the national FIU that receives your reporting.

VASP & crypto
NBG Maintains the register of virtual asset service providers. VASPs enter through NBG registration, not a bank-style licence, and a register entry buys no relief from the full AML obligation. Public VASP register at nbg.gov.ge.
Payments & MSB
NBG Supervises payment and money-services businesses under a licensing regime. Supervisory information at nbg.gov.ge.
Banks & FIs
NBG Licenses and oversees commercial banks and other financial institutions. List of licensed commercial banks at nbg.gov.ge.
Funds & CSP
NBG Supervises investment funds and fund administration. Investment-funds supervision page at nbg.gov.ge.
Gold & DPMS
MoF Carries AML supervision for persons trading in precious stones and metals, the closest accountable category for gold, bullion and high-value dealing.
Gaming
Revenue Service A legal entity of public law under the Ministry of Finance. Supervises gaming and gambling operators, and issues permits that sit alongside export-control decisions.
Real estate & DNFBP
NAPR / MoJBarSARAS No single dedicated regulator. Property gatekeeping runs through the National Agency of Public Registry (NAPR) under the Ministry of Justice, at napr.gov.ge. Professional AML is split: MoJ for notaries, the Georgian Bar Association for lawyers, SARAS for accountants and auditors.
Defence & dual-use
MoDRevenue Service Exports controlled by the Ministry of Defence through its Standing Committee on Military-Technical Matters. Permits issued by the Revenue Service.
Art & high-value
MoFFMS Not separately enumerated as an accountable category. Closest accountable footing is persons trading in precious stones or metals, AML-supervised by the Ministry of Finance. Do not assume you fall outside scope: map to the nearest enumerated category and build to it.
Across every accountable sector, the Financial Monitoring Service of Georgia (FMS) is the national FIU: the body that receives your suspicious-transaction and threshold reporting. We ground every reference in the current published framework, and never assert a deadline, penalty or statute number without a source.
03Who operates here, and what they build

Licensed or only registered, every operator here is an accountable person under Georgian AML law and must build and run the same core, sized to its risk. A register entry buys no relief. Who Georgia draws:

  • Virtual asset firms on the NBG registration regime.
  • Payment and e-money businesses on the Caucasus corridor.
  • Banks and microfinance institutions.
  • Fund and administration vehicles.
  • Gaming operators, bullion dealers and property intermediaries.
