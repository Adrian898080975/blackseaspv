What sits behind the licence

The licence is the easy part. We build what sits behind it.

That is the programme a supervisor examines: the business risk assessment, policies and procedures, customer due diligence, screening and monitoring rules, reporting to the FIU, training, the MLRO's reports and the board pack.

Black Sea is an advisory firm and sells no software. Your systems stay your own, and we write the rules and tests they have to meet.

Why firms bring us in

Written to your rulebook

Each programme is written against the rulebook your supervisor examines, for your licence category and your market. A virtual asset firm in Dubai outside the DIFC answers to VARA, and one inside the DIFC answers to the DFSA, so their programmes are written to different texts.

One fixed fee per stage

You commit to one stage at a time. The costed plan states the fee for each stage, what it includes and what it leaves out.

Independent of your team and your vendors

We sell only our own work, so a finding of ours is never a sales lead. We never audit a programme we built or run.

Senior practitioners, with law firms in market

The practitioners who scope your work deliver it. Where a point turns on local law, a law firm in your market advises under its own engagement.
