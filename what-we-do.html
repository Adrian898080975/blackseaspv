Licensing & new-regime build

The AML/CFT and sanctions programme your application needs, in place before the supervisor asks for it.

Get a costed plan

You receive

  • Business risk assessment
  • Policies and procedures mapped to your rulebook
  • Customer risk scoring and due diligence
  • Screening and transaction monitoring rules
  • Travel rule procedure, for virtual asset firms
  • MLRO framework and reporting lines
  • Record keeping
  • Staff and board training
  • Registration with the FIU, on goAML where the FIU uses it

  • For

    firms applying for a licence or a registration, entering a new market, or brought into scope by a new law.

  • When

    before the application is filed, or when a new regime sets its start date.

  • How it starts

  • Stays with your law firm

Remediation

The repair a finding requires, closed with evidence the supervisor can test.

Get a costed plan

You receive

  • Gap analysis against each finding
  • Remediation plan with owners and dates
  • Rebuilt controls, policies and procedures
  • Look-back reviews, where the finding calls for one
  • Evidence pack for each finding, ready for the supervisor's follow-up

  • For

    firms with a finding from a supervisor, an external auditor or a correspondent bank.

  • When

    after an inspection, a supervisory letter, a qualified audit or a bank's request, usually with a deadline.

  • Time

    worked back from the deadline in the finding.

  • How it starts

    you send the finding under NDA, and we reply with a costed plan.

  • Stays with you and your counsel

    representation before the supervisor. We prepare the evidence you submit.

Independent AML audit

A test of whether your programme works in practice, reported to your board.

Get a costed plan

You receive

  • Testing of the programme's design
  • Testing of how it operates, on a sample of real files
  • Screening and monitoring tests
  • Governance review
  • Board report with rated findings and a dated action list

  • For

    boards and audit committees, firms whose rulebook calls for a periodic independent review, and investors assessing a target.

  • When

    the review falls due, a supervisor or a bank asks for one, an inspection is coming, or a transaction needs it.

  • Time

    set by the size of the file sample and the date your report is due.

  • How it starts

    we do not audit a programme we built or run, and we sell none of the systems we test.

How we work

A build or a remediation runs in three stages, and you commit to one stage at a time. An audit, a diligence file or a perimeter review runs as one stage, at one fixed fee.

StageWhenWhat you hold at the end
AssessWeeks 1 and 2A written gap report against your rulebook, and a fixed fee for the build
BuildWeeks 3 to 8The documents, rules and training the report found missing, in place and approved by your management
OperateFrom go-liveOfficer cover, ongoing support and periodic testing, monthly

The costed plan sets the actual weeks. If you go ahead with the build, the assessment fee is credited against it, on the terms the plan states.

How the fee is set

One fixed fee for each stage, with what it includes and leaves out. We do not bill by the hour; a change of scope is agreed in writing before work on it starts.

Why an outside firm

A review is independent only when someone outside your team carries it out. A build or an empty seat takes hours your team needs for its own work: we take that work and hand it back finished.

Ways to engage

  • A project with a fixed end: a build, a remediation, an audit or a diligence file.
  • A practitioner beside your team on a defined gap, while your officer keeps the function.
  • A standing function under your board: officer cover or ongoing support, monthly.

What we need from you

An NDA, your licence stage and any finding or deadline, access to your documents and people once the NDA is signed, and one person who can approve the scope.

Who does the work

The practitioners who scope your work deliver it, from the assessment to the handover.

Confidentiality

We sign an NDA before you send detail. We never publish client names, and one client's file is never another client's reference.
