Independent AML audit
Testing must be independent of those who run the programme. We are that reviewer, and only that.
What we test, build and deliver
Trigger
A periodic independent-review obligation, or a board wanting assurance before an examination.

A genuinely independent review that tested controls in operation, not policy on the page.

A scoped audit: design and operating-effectiveness testing, file sampling, screening validation, governance review.
Deliverable
A board-ready report: tested findings, ratings and a prioritised action list.
For
Boards, audit committees, operators meeting a periodic-review obligation, and investors assessing a target.

05
Sanctions, export-control and integrity due diligence
Screening a name against a list is not due diligence. We resolve ownership, clear the corridor, check cargo, and build the FATF Travel Rule into transfers.
What we test, build and deliver
Trigger
A counterparty or corridor to clear, a shipment to check, or a transfer needing Travel Rule data.
What the examiner tests
Ownership resolved through opaque structures, exposure assessed against the relevant regimes, data travelling with each transfer.
What we build
Enhanced sanctions and integrity diligence, beneficial-ownership resolution, export-control and dual-use screening, Travel Rule implementation.
Deliverable
A sourced diligence file with a clear risk conclusion, plus a live Travel Rule control where relevant.
For
Operators onboarding high-risk counterparties, VASPs implementing the Travel Rule, and firms with export-control exposure.
