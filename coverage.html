How we work in your market

Senior practitioners lead each engagement, and law firms in the market take the local legal questions under their own engagement.

Most of the work is delivered remotely. Where a supervisor or the work itself needs someone on site, the costed plan says when and for how long.

Where a market allows an outside MLRO, we can provide one for an agreed period. Where the seat must be held by your own employee, or by a citizen or a resident of the market, your appointee holds it and we stand behind that person.

Bahrain

In Bahrain, the supervisor depends on your licence. The AML/CFT law is Decree-Law No. (4) of 2001, as amended.

Kuwait

In Kuwait, the supervisor depends on your licence. The AML/CFT law is Law No. (106) of 2013, as amended, and Ministerial Resolution No. (37) of 2013 issued its executive regulation.

Oman

In Oman, the supervisor depends on your licence. The AML/CFT law is the Law on Combating Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing, promulgated by Royal Decree No. 30/2016.

Read the full brief

Qatar

Qatar has two perimeters. Firms in the Qatar Financial Centre (QFC) answer to the Qatar Financial Centre Regulatory Authority (QFCRA), and firms onshore answer to national supervisors. Both are subject to Law No. (20) of 2019 on Combating Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing, amended by Law No. (18) of 2025, and to its Implementing Regulations, promulgated by Council of Ministers' Decision No. (41) of 2019.

Read the full brief

Saudi Arabia

In Saudi Arabia, the supervisor depends on your licence. Money laundering duties come from the Anti-Money Laundering Law, issued by Royal Decree No. (M/20) in 2017 and since amended, and from its implementing regulation. Terrorist financing falls under a separate law, issued by Royal Decree No. (M/21).

Read the full brief

United Arab Emirates

In the UAE, the supervisor depends on your licence and on where you hold it. The federal AML/CFT law is Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025, and Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025 sets out its executive regulations.

Egypt

In Egypt, the supervisor depends on your licence or your business. The AML/CFT law is the Anti-Money Laundering Law promulgated by Law No. 80 of 2002, with its amendments, and its executive regulations were issued by Prime Ministerial Decision No. 951 of 2003.

Jordan

In Jordan, the supervisor depends on your licence or your business. The AML/CFT law is Law No. (20) of 2021.

Africa

Ghana

In Ghana, the supervisor depends on your licence or your business. The AML/CFT law is the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2020 (Act 1044).

Kenya

In Kenya, the Financial Reporting Centre (FRC) supervises all reporting institutions, and each supervisory body also supervises the firms it regulates. The AML/CFT law is the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act, Cap. 59A (POCAMLA), with the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Regulations, 2023 (Legal Notice 153 of 2023).

Nigeria

In Nigeria, the supervisor depends on your licence or your business. The AML/CFT law is the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act, 2022.

Read the full brief

South Africa

In South Africa, the supervisor depends on your business. The AML/CFT law is the Financial Intelligence Centre Act, 2001 (Act 38 of 2001), the FIC Act, amended by the General Laws (Anti-Money Laundering and Combating Terrorism Financing) Amendment Act, 2022 (Act No. 22 of 2022). Schedule 2 of the FIC Act names a supervisory body for some kinds of accountable institution, and the Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC) supervises the others.

Azerbaijan

In Azerbaijan, the supervisor depends on your licence or your profession. The AML/CFT law is Law No. 781-VIQ of 30 December 2022.

Georgia

In Georgia, the supervisor depends on your licence or your profession. The AML/CFT law is the Law of Georgia on Facilitating the Prevention of Money Laundering and the Financing of Terrorism, of 30 October 2019.

Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan has two perimeters. Firms in the Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC) answer to the Astana Financial Services Authority (AFSA) under the AIFC AML rules, and firms onshore answer to national supervisors. The national AML law is Law No. 191-IV of 28 August 2009.

Kyrgyzstan

In Kyrgyzstan, the supervisor depends on your licence, and the government sets which body examines which firm. The AML/CFT law is Law No. 87 of 6 August 2018 "On countering the financing of criminal activity and the legalisation (laundering) of criminal proceeds", and Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 739 of 14 November 2025 approved the regulations that implement it.

Read the full brief

Uzbekistan

In Uzbekistan, the supervisor depends on your licence. The AML/CFT law is Law No. 660-II of 26 August 2004.

Anatolia & South Asia

Turkey

In Turkey, the supervisor depends on your licence, and MASAK, the Financial Crimes Investigation Board of the Ministry of Treasury and Finance, receives suspicious transaction reports and imposes fines. Under the anti-money laundering law, Law No. 5549 of 11 October 2006 on Prevention of Laundering Proceeds of Crime, compliance is examined by the examiners it lists, among them experts of the Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BDDK) and the Capital Markets Board (CMB) and auditors and experts of the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye.

Read the full brief

Pakistan

In Pakistan, your AML/CFT supervisor depends on your licence or your profession. Schedule IV of the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2010 (the AML Act) names the regulators and self-regulatory bodies that act as AML/CFT regulatory authorities, and the Virtual Assets Act, 2026 set up a regulator to license and supervise virtual asset service providers.

Read the full brief

Americas

United States

In the United States, the supervisor depends on your business. The AML/CFT law is the Bank Secrecy Act (31 U.S.C. 5311 and following), with its rules in Chapter X of Title 31 of the Code of Federal Regulations. The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN), a bureau of the Treasury, administers the Act, delegates the examination of firms to the agencies below and keeps the power to impose civil penalties.

Read the full brief

Canada

In Canada, every business in the table below has the same supervisor. The AML/CFT law is the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (S.C. 2000, c. 17), with its regulations. The Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC) is Canada's financial intelligence unit and its anti-money laundering and anti-terrorist financing supervisor, and the Act charges it with ensuring compliance.

Read the full brief

Cayman Islands

In the Cayman Islands, the supervisor depends on your business. The AML/CFT rules for businesses are set out in the Anti-Money Laundering Regulations (AMLRs), made under the Proceeds of Crime Act (POCA), and they apply to every business that carries on relevant financial business.

Read the full brief

Europe

United Kingdom

In the United Kingdom, the supervisor depends on your business. The AML/CFT rules for businesses are the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017, and the duty to report suspicion comes from the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 and the Terrorism Act 2000.

Read the full brief

Switzerland

In Switzerland, the supervisor depends on your business. The AML/CFT law is the Federal Act on Combating Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (Anti-Money Laundering Act, AMLA).

Read the full brief

Asia-Pacific

Singapore

In Singapore, the supervisor depends on your business. The duty to report suspicious transactions comes from the Corruption, Drug Trafficking and Other Serious Crimes (Confiscation of Benefits) Act 1992. The other AML/CFT rules come from the legislation for each sector and, for the financial institutions it supervises, from the AML/CFT notices of the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS).

Read the full brief

Hong Kong

In Hong Kong, the supervisor depends on your business. The main AML/CFT law is the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615, AMLO), which sets customer due diligence and record-keeping duties for financial institutions and for designated non-financial businesses and professions. The duty to report suspicious transactions comes from the Drug Trafficking (Recovery of Proceeds) Ordinance (Cap. 405), the Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 455) and the United Nations (Anti-Terrorism Measures) Ordinance (Cap. 575).

Read the full brief

Australia

In Australia, every business in the table below has the same supervisor. The AML/CFT law is the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (the AML/CTF Act), and the AML/CTF Rules set out how its obligations are carried out. The Australian Transaction Reports and Analysis Centre (AUSTRAC) is Australia's anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing regulator and its financial intelligence unit.
