02Your obligation here

South Africa supervises virtual-asset activity through two bodies working in tandem: the Financial Sector Conduct Authority licenses crypto asset service providers under its financial-services framework, and the Financial Intelligence Centre supervises the AML/CFT obligations and receives the reports. The obligation behind the licence is consistent in shape: a named compliance officer, a written AML/CFT programme, a documented risk assessment, risk-based customer due diligence, ongoing sanctions and PEP screening, transaction monitoring, and suspicious-transaction reporting to the Financial Intelligence Centre.

Conduct & licensing
FSCA The Financial Sector Conduct Authority licenses crypto asset service providers under its financial-services framework. Register: fsca.co.za/Fais/Search_FSP.htm
AML/CFT & reporting

04Why it is urgent now
Standing exposure
Virtual assets in South Africa are already supervised. The FSCA licenses the activity, the FIC supervises the AML side and receives the reports, and the register of licensed providers is public, so an operator without a defensible AML/CFT programme is exposed at the next examination, not at some future deadline. Cross-border virtual-asset flows put the operator squarely in the sanctions-screening line of sight, which is where thin programmes fail first. The pressure is standing, not seasonal.
