There is no single door in Oman

Oman splits supervision by sector across a small set of authorities. Know which one holds your file before a single control is designed. Two distinctions matter:

  • The door depends on the perimeter: FSA holds virtual-asset, funds and corporate-service; CBO holds banking, payments and money-services; MOCIIP holds DNFBP.
  • Some activity is not licensed at all. Art and high-value goods fall under the general DNFBP and cash-threshold regime, not a bespoke licence. Gaming is prohibited, so the honest work is exposure and blocking-regime awareness, not an authorisation that does not exist.
  • The expectation is consistent whoever holds your file: a risk assessment you can defend, controls that match it, a named officer accountable for them, and reporting to the NCFI when the framework requires it.

VASP & crypto
FSACBO The Financial Services Authority authorises and supervises the capital-markets, funds and virtual-asset perimeter, and maintains a public register of authorised and accredited firms. Where payments intersect crypto, the Central Bank of Oman's perimeter is engaged as well.
Payments & MSB
CBO The Central Bank of Oman supervises the payments and money-services perimeter and maintains a public register of licensed institutions.
Banks & FIs
CBO The Central Bank of Oman supervises banks, financing and leasing institutions, listed on the CBO's institutions register.
Funds & CSP
FSA The FSA holds the funds, fund-administration and corporate-service file, with authorised firms on the FSA public register.
Gold & DPMS
MOCIIPDGSM The Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion is the DNFBP AML/CFT supervisor for dealers in precious metals and stones. Hallmarking and assaying sit with the Directorate General for Standards and Metrology. Supervision and metrology are two different things.
Gaming
ROPTRA Gambling is prohibited under Omani law. There is no gaming regulator to license: enforcement sits with the Public Prosecution and the Royal Oman Police, and online sites are blocked by the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority. The honest work here is exposure mapping, not licensing.
Real estate & DNFBP
MHUPMOCIIPNCFI The Ministry of Housing and Urban Planning licenses and registers the real-estate broker profession. MOCIIP is the DNFBP AML/CFT supervisor. Suspicious transaction reports run to the National Centre for Financial Information, the national FIU.
Defence & dual-use
ROP CustomsMOCIIP Export and dual-use goods control runs through the Royal Oman Police Directorate General of Customs alongside MOCIIP. This is export-control and proliferation-financing exposure, not classic AML supervision.
Art & high-value
MOCIIPNCFI No dedicated regulator. Art and high-value-goods dealers are not a separately scheduled DNFBP sector in Oman: AML expectations fall under the general DNFBP and cash-threshold regime supervised by MOCIIP, with STRs to the NCFI. The absence of a bespoke supervisor does not reduce the obligation.
