We do no legal work, so a client you introduce stays your client for everything legal.

How a referral works

  1. We sign an NDA and a cooperation agreement with your firm.
  2. You introduce the client, or describe the case without names.
  3. We send a costed plan to the client, or to you if you prefer, within 48 hours of your message.
  4. Each firm agrees its own scope and fee with the client, and the legal work stays with you.
  5. Where a client of ours needs counsel in your market, we can introduce your firm.

Confidentiality

Nothing about your client moves before the NDA. We name a partner firm only with its consent.
