About

Operator-side.
Not consultancy-side.

Black Sea is an independent financial-crime compliance boutique. We are not a law firm and we are not the Big Four. We are the senior, conflict-free specialist that builds the programme behind a licence, and the one a regulator can trust to have built it properly.

§01How we work

Five rules we don't break.

01
We've sat the seat
 Our people have built and run regulated structures, not just advised on them. The advice is what we would have wanted when we were on the other side of the table, facing the examiner.
02
Clear scope, no hourly billing
 Every engagement starts with a fixed-scope diagnostic and is costed up front on the call. Hourly billing rewards slowness, so we do not use it. No surprises, no scope creep.
03
Lawyers file, we build
 For matters that need a regulated legal opinion we work alongside partner counsel in the right jurisdiction. They own the licensing file. We build the compliance programme that has to sit behind it. We never compete with the firm that refers us.
04
Independent and conflict-free
 We sell no software and take no commissions, so the programme we build is the one you need, not the one that pays us. We screen beneficial ownership and sanctions before we engage, and we say no when we should.
05
Senior only
 You get the people you met on the call. No pyramid of juniors learning on your file, and no name-partner who disappears after the pitch.
§02Who we are

Built by people who have run the function.

Black Sea was founded by Adrián Páleš. §03The lane

We build the programme, and the way through.

We do not write legal opinions and we do not sell a platform. What we do is build, remediate and run the AML and CFT programme, and the sanctions and export-control layer, behind a licence. And because compliance is never separate from the business, we help you shape the strategy and find the solution: which licence, which jurisdiction, which structure, and how to get through the regime in front of you. That focus is the whole point. It is why a law firm refers to us without fear, and why a regulator takes the work seriously.
