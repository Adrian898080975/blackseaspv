§03How we work

Operator-side. Not consultancy-side.

Three rules shape every engagement. They explain why clients pick us over a Big Four advisory or a traditional law firm — and why we say no to work outside our scope.

01

We’ve sat the seat.

Our team has built and run regulated structures, not just advised on them. We have received the regulator findings, drafted the response, sat the inspection, hired the compliance officer.

That experience changes the advice. You do not get a generic framework adapted to your situation. You get the answer to a question we already had to solve on our own balance sheet.

Consultancy-side advice tells you what should be done. Operator-side advice tells you what can be done, in what order, with what evidence the regulator will accept.

02

Clear scope, fixed price.

We do not bill by the hour. Hourly billing creates incentives we do not want — to slow down, to over-research, to lengthen.

Every engagement starts with a two-week diagnostic. At the end you receive a scoped proposal: deliverables, timeline, fixed fee. You decide whether to proceed. If you do, the price does not move.

For ongoing relationships we work on monthly retainer. Fixed fee, defined scope, clear escalation path for matters that exceed it.

03

Licensed counsel when needed.

We are an advisory practice, not a law firm. For matters that require a regulated legal opinion, signed counsel, or admission to a regulator’s register, we retain partner counsel in the right jurisdiction.

You see the cost. You approve the engagement. The partner reports through us so you have one point of accountability, not three.

This is the line we do not blur. We are honest about what advisory can do and what it cannot, and we structure engagements so you are never paying us for something a licensed lawyer should be doing.

§04Engagement

What it looks like in practice.

Format
Project-based or monthly retainer. Project work is the default for new clients. Retainer once we have completed at least one project and the working relationship is calibrated.
Cycle
Two weeks for a diagnostic. Two to six months for a full mandate. We do not start work without a written scope, and we do not extend a project without a written change request.
Range
Diagnostics from €3K. Structuring mandates €15K to €75K. Retainers from €5K monthly. Quoted as fixed price. We do not invoice for travel, materials, or filing fees without disclosing them up front.
Communication
One sender, weekly check-in, written summaries. You do not chase us. We send a written update every Friday with progress, blockers, and the next week’s scope.
Conflicts
We disclose every potential conflict in writing before engagement. If we cannot take a mandate because of an existing relationship, we say so. We do not run dual mandates without explicit waiver from both parties.

Send us one paragraph.

Tell us what you are trying to structure or solve. We respond within 48 hours with a yes, a no, or a question.
